Architecture overview

A conversation is redacted on arrival, reviewed once settled, then deleted

Two clocks are at work. Text arrives turn by turn, and extraction waits until the conversation has gone quiet.

The steps

  1. The adapter posts new turns to /transcript. Secrets are redacted before anything reaches disk, and nothing is buffered if capture is off (packages/mcp-server/src/http/transcript-intake.ts).
  2. A buffer settles after 30 minutes idle, an explicit end, or at 5 MB. The sweep claims it by renaming it to .processing (packages/core/src/transcript-sweep.ts:93-97,361-363).
  3. The extractor makes one model pass and returns discrete candidate facts (packages/core/src/transcript-extract.ts).
  4. The reviewer reads the transcript and judges each fact. A kept or fixed fact needs a quote that is really in the transcript (packages/core/src/transcript-review.ts).
  5. Surviving facts are redacted again and submitted to the inbox, one each.
  6. Separately, a journal step writes the conversation's entry. Each step records its own success, so a retry repeats only what failed.
  7. The buffer is deleted. Intake files the inbox on its own timer, as on the curator page.
Why review facts straight after extraction?
Decision
A second call judges each fact against the transcript: keep, fix or drop. It judges only what the extractor wrote.
Constraint
The extractor overproduces, and this is the last moment the source exists. Rewriting can introduce errors, so quotes are checked in code.
Rejected
Having it hunt for missed facts: that repeats the extractor's job.
Cost accepted
One more model call per conversation that yields facts. It can only check a fact against the conversation, not against the code.
Source
Spec 086, in the maintainer's private notes; packages/core/src/transcript-review.ts (observed)
Why is the transcript deleted?
Decision
Raw transcripts are removed after their steps finish and live outside the git vault.
Constraint
A buffer is high-churn raw conversation. Committing it would bloat git history and keep the conversation there permanently, so only extracted facts reach the committed path.
Rejected
Committing buffers to the vault. Keeping raw transcripts as a fallback for searching: left out of the journal's first version, to be revisited after it had run for a month.
Cost accepted
Nothing later can check a memory against what was actually said, and journal search sees only what survived the roll-ups.
Source
Capture brainstorm and spec 077, in the maintainer's private notes; transcript-intake.ts (observed)
Why do captured facts go through the same inbox as remember?
Decision
Extraction adds an entrance in front of the existing inbox, not a second filing system.
Constraint
The inbox and curator already dedupe, merge and file.
Rejected
A separate extraction system.
Cost accepted
Filing is asynchronous, so a fact isn't recallable the moment it is said.
Source
ADR 0009 (observed)

Failure keeps the conversation and retries, up to a limit

What fails What happens
Extraction or review, attempts 1 and 2 Buffer kept and retried after the reaper window of one hour. The retry re-runs extraction.
Review, last attempt The extractor's facts are filed unreviewed.
Extraction, attempt 3 The conversation is dropped.
Provider unavailable The sweep stops, so requests don't pile up behind a struggling model.
Journal step Doesn't affect facts already filed; it retries on its own.

Source: packages/core/src/transcript-sweep.ts (facts and failure handling in runTranscriptSweepTick); DEFAULT_TRANSCRIPT_MAX_ATTEMPTS is 3.

Known limit: a claim nobody challenged in the conversation will pass review, and the reviewer has only been tested on one local model.
