Architecture overview

One curator files new facts and tidies old ones under a single apply rule

Intake handles what's new, and grooming improves what's already there. Both ask a configured OpenAI-compatible model what to do, and code decides whether that answer is allowed to take effect.

Confidence decides whether a person sees it

An operation applies at or above curator.apply.confidence_threshold, which defaults to 0.8, and otherwise becomes a proposal. A noop is skipped. The rule is the same for intake and grooming.

Why one threshold for everything?
Decision
Create, update, merge, split and archive all follow one rule, with no exceptions.
Constraint
Archive and split used to reach review queues regardless of confidence, so a threshold of 0 didn't mean what the setting said.
Rejected
Per-operation settings: left out until practice shows they're needed.
Cost accepted
Default installs see more unattended changes, including confident archives and splits.
Source
ADR 0014 (observed); packages/core/src/curator-apply-policy.ts:44
Why check the model's writes in code?
Decision
After the threshold says apply, deterministic guards refuse unsafe writes, including any new body over 4,000 characters.
Constraint
A confident model can still write something unsafe, and memories must stay small enough for grooming to read whole.
Rejected
Relying on the confidence score alone.
Cost accepted
Secondary facts in a bundled submission could go unfiled; intake later gained derived items for them.
Source
ADR 0015 (observed); packages/core/src/curator-guards.ts:15, packages/core/src/store/corpus/inbox.ts

Grooming runs in bounded slices

A run gathers evidence for a slice of memories, does a deterministic pre-pass, asks the model, then validates and applies the operations. Slices whose input hasn't changed are skipped.

Trigger When
schedule The configured interval and time of day
post_intake Enough memories changed since the last groom, outside the debounce window
flag 10 minutes after the latest flag, and at most 30 after the first
manual Run now, from the dashboard

Source: packages/core/src/grooming-worker.ts, packages/core/src/grooming-tick.ts, packages/core/src/grooming-trigger.ts, ADR 0013.

Why do flags go through grooming?
Decision
flag_memory only records the flag, and a targeted groom makes the correction.
Constraint
The earlier separate correction path could only delete exact quoted text and duplicated proposal-review code.
Rejected
The separate correction worker (ADR 0013 supersedes ADR 0012).
Cost accepted
The model writes a whole new body, which is broader than a deletion, so it needs its own audit and truncation guards.
Source
ADR 0013 (observed)

Known limits

Memory quality depends on the configured model. The curator can only judge what it is shown: it does not read your repository. A persistently failing model retries items until they are parked in inbox/.failed/ after their third failure.
